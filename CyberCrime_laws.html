LEGAL QUICK REFERENCE 10 TOPICS

Cybercrime & Digital Privacy

A student-friendly overview of key provisions and official reporting options in India.

01

COMPUTER RESOURCES

Unauthorized Access & Damage

Section 43 Act Information Technology Act, 2000

Brief Covers specified unauthorized access, copying, damage, disruption, or interference involving computer resources.

Example Someone accesses another student's laptop without permission and copies private files.

Remember Don't access without permission.

02

DIGITAL IDENTITY

Identity Theft

Section 66C Act Information Technology Act, 2000

Brief Fraudulent or dishonest use of another person's specified electronic identification information can be an offence.

Example Someone uses your password to enter your account.

Remember Protect your digital identity.

03

ONLINE FRAUD

Online Personation

Section 66D Act Information Technology Act, 2000

Brief Cheating by personation using a computer resource or communication device can be an offence.

Example A fake account pretends to be your friend and asks for money.

Remember Verify before you trust.

04

PRIVACY & CONSENT

Violation of Privacy

Section 66E Act Information Technology Act, 2000

Brief Deals with specified non-consensual capture, publication, or transmission of images of a person's private area.

Example Someone secretly captures and shares a private image.

Remember Respect privacy and consent.

05

ONLINE CONTENT

Obscene Material Online

Section 67 Act Information Technology Act, 2000

Brief Deals with publishing or transmitting obscene material in electronic form, subject to legal requirements.

Example Someone deliberately uploads prohibited obscene material.

Remember Think before you upload.

06

ONLINE CONTENT

Sexually Explicit Material

Section 67A Act Information Technology Act, 2000

Brief Deals with publishing or transmitting sexually explicit material electronically, subject to legal requirements.

Example Someone deliberately forwards sexually explicit content in a group.

Remember Don't share unlawful content.

07

PERSONAL SAFETY

Stalking

Section 78 Act Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

Brief Covers stalking, including specified electronic monitoring/contact of a woman.

Example Someone repeatedly contacts a woman online after clear disinterest.

Remember Respect digital boundaries.

08

THREATS

Criminal Intimidation

Section 351 Act Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

Brief Deals with threats made with the intent and circumstances specified by the provision.

Example Someone sends serious threatening messages to cause alarm.

Remember Online threats can have consequences.

09

CYBERSECURITY

Cybersecurity & CERT-In

Section 70B Act Information Technology Act, 2000

Brief Provides for CERT-In and specified national cybersecurity incident-response functions.

Example A major cyber incident requires coordinated response.

Remember Report and respond responsibly.

10

GET HELP

Cybercrime Reporting

Mechanism Official mechanism Authority Government of India

Brief The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal provides an official channel for reporting cybercrime; 1930 is used for cyber financial fraud reporting.

Example A student reports an online payment scam immediately.

Remember STOP → SECURE → SAVE → REPORT.

These are brief educational summaries, not legal advice. Applicability depends on the facts and current law.

Official legislation source: India Code
