Consumer Rights

A student-friendly overview of consumer protections and support options in India.

Take the Consumer Rights quiz

01

PRODUCT SAFETY

Right to Safety

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Protects consumers against goods and services hazardous to life and property.

Example A buyer checks safety information before buying an appliance.

Remember Choose safe products.

02

PRODUCT INFORMATION

Right to Information

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Gives consumers a right to relevant information about goods and services.

Example A buyer checks MRP, expiry date, ingredients, and quantity.

Remember Know before you buy.

03

INFORMED CHOICE

Right to Choose

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Supports access to a variety of goods and services at competitive prices.

Example A student compares brands before buying a phone.

Remember Compare your options.

04

COMPLAINTS

Right to Be Heard

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Consumer interests should receive due consideration through appropriate forums and mechanisms.

Example A customer raises a complaint about a defective product.

Remember Your voice matters.

05

CONSUMER REMEDIES

Right to Seek Redressal

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Allows consumers to seek remedies for eligible defects, deficiencies, and unfair practices.

Example A consumer keeps the invoice and seeks an appropriate remedy.

Remember Keep your proof.

06

AWARENESS

Right to Consumer Awareness

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Recognizes consumer awareness/education as a consumer right.

Example Students learn to spot misleading discounts.

Remember Stay informed.

07

ADVERTISING

Misleading Advertisements

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Provides protections concerning misleading advertisements and unfair practices.

Example An advertisement makes a false product claim.

Remember Check claims before buying.

08

FAIR TRANSACTIONS

Unfair Trade Practices

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Addresses specified unfair trade practices in consumer transactions.

Example A seller uses deceptive methods to influence a purchase.

Remember Watch for unfair practices.

09

PRODUCT SAFETY

Product Liability

Section / Act Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Brief Provides a framework for product-liability claims in specified circumstances.

Example A defective product causes harm and the consumer explores applicable remedies.

Remember Know your remedies.

10

GET HELP

National Consumer Helpline

Channel Official support channel Authority Government of India

Brief Provides consumer guidance and grievance assistance through the official consumer helpline system.

Example A student seeks guidance after an online seller ignores a complaint.

Remember Keep records and seek help.

These are brief educational summaries, not legal advice. Applicability depends on the facts and current law.
